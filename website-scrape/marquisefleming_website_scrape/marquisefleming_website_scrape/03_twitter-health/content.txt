01 - DISCOVERY
--------------
Knowledge Learning:
During discovery with Twitter stakeholders, we gained critical insights into their commitment to fostering a safe and healthy platform for public discourse. Stakeholders emphasized the need for transparency, user trust, and proactive measures to address safety concerns. We learned about their desire to create tools and processes that empower both users and employees to mitigate risks and promote healthier interactions. Collaboration revealed the importance of balancing swift responses to crises with long-term cultural and systemic improvements. These insights shaped our design direction, ensuring alignment with Twitter's vision and stakeholder priorities.

02 - RESEARCH
-------------
Knowledge Learning:
During our research phase, we discovered a recurring theme among diverse user groups: many people did not feel safe on Twitter. We conducted interviews and focus groups with a broad range of communities, including LGBTQ individuals, Black people, White people, Middle-class families, low-income users, affluent individuals in the 1%, Christians, Catholics, Asians, Americans, Jewish people, and others. Across the board, participants expressed concerns about harassment, misinformation, and a lack of protection from harmful interactions. This highlighted a significant gap between the platform's intent to foster open dialogue and users' experiences of safety. It became clear that safety and trust are not universal concepts—they are deeply personal and shaped by individual identities and experiences.

[RESEARCH METRICS IMAGE: image_02_d29t8ayKLEefJ5I1rp1HUJmDIk.png - Research statistics]

Research Metrics:
- 25 User Interviews
- 30+ User Group Types
- 5 Stakeholder Interviews
- 100 Data points analyzed

Key Finding:
One of the most striking findings was the sense of isolation many users felt. Participants expressed a desire for tailored support groups or spaces where their unique challenges and concerns could be addressed in a more focused and affirming way. For example, LGBTQ users sought stronger moderation against homophobic content, while religious groups requested safeguards against faith-based attacks. These insights underscored the importance of creating not just universal safety measures but also targeted initiatives that resonate with different communities. This need for community-specific support informed our approach, ensuring that safety solutions would empower users to connect while feeling seen, heard, and protected.

07 - DEFINING A DESIGN LANGUAGE
--------------------------------
Supportive:
Twitter faced a significant challenge in creating a cohesive brand identity around "Twitter Health." The initiative aimed to position the platform as a safe and inclusive digital space, emphasizing its commitment to cracking down on hate speech and disruptive behaviors. However, the lack of a clear narrative and visual identity left users and stakeholders questioning the platform's efforts. This project focused on establishing Twitter Health as a recognizable and trustworthy movement, demonstrating the company's dedication to fostering healthy, meaningful interactions online.

#HandsOn - Supportive, Caring, Fair:

07 - WHERE WE LANDED
--------------------
Arbiter:
We developed a design style that set a tone of warmth and fairness, striking the balance between kindness and authority. By adopting a straightforward yet approachable aesthetic, we positioned Twitter Health as a strong, neutral decision-maker. This style conveyed unbiased consideration while fostering trust and encouraging users to respect the rules of engagement.

[FINAL DESIGN IMAGE: image_15_R9n5RMQfXM176LVvfuBS6y5sw.png - Final design showcase]
[FINAL DESIGN IMAGE: image_16_b0GLR9gr3a2EVklIL1D0nlewG0.png - Design application 1]
[FINAL DESIGN IMAGE: image_17_sg4qwCxzpsKAwem0NfAlrqXVfw.png - Design application 2]

#Arbiter - Empathetic, Bold, Communal:
When users see Twitter as a fair and neutral decision-maker, they're more likely to respect the rules of engagement. We'll use language that is kind, but that stays above the fray. And a straightforward tone that projects strong, independent, unbiased consideration.

Informed:
Our language conveys knowledge, certainty, and trustworthiness. We communicate clear stances on our policies, and we use our platform to uphold and reinforce the standards of the community.

Kind:
With concern for every user in mind, we provide ways of quietly stepping away from situations that may feel physically or psychologically unsafe, allowing breathing room away from those interactions.
